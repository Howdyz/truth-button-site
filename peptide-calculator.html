Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate peptide reconstitution?

Concentration equals the peptide amount in the vial divided by the volume of liquid (bacteriostatic water) you add. Once you know the concentration, dose volume equals your target dose divided by that concentration.

Is this calculator medical advice?

No. It's a math tool that converts a dose you already have — from a prescription, protocol, or product label — into a volume you can measure. It doesn't tell you what dose to take. Follow your prescriber's or product's dosing instructions.

Is this calculator free?

Yes, completely free, no account required.

Being straight about this: this calculator does simple arithmetic (concentration = amount ÷ volume; dose volume = dose ÷ concentration; syringe units = dose volume × syringe scale). It can't catch a typo, a wrong vial size, or a unit mix-up. Talk to a licensed healthcare provider before using any peptide — many sold as "research use only" aren't evaluated by the FDA for human use. This tool has no idea what peptide you're calculating for and makes no claim about safety, efficacy, or appropriate dosing for any substance.
