Frequently asked questions

How do I record music playing in a browser tab?

Open Lightning Downloader, click the share button, then pick the tab, window, or screen playing the music or audio in your browser's own sharing dialog — make sure "Share tab audio" or "Share system audio" is checked. Once the source is ready, click Start Recording.

What format does it save as?

MP3 at 320kbps, encoded locally in your browser right after you stop recording — no separate conversion step.

Does this upload my recording anywhere?

No. Recording, MP3 encoding, and playback all happen locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server — the file only exists on your device until you download it.

Is this free?

The first 5 minutes of recording are free, no account required. After that, a one-time $10 payment unlocks unlimited recording forever — there's no subscription.

Can I record from Spotify or Apple Music web players?

Yes, if the tab is playing audible sound, this tool captures it like any other tab audio — but you're responsible for only recording content you have the right to record, under that service's own terms.

What's the maximum length of a single recording?

There's no hard technical cap after the $10 unlock — length is limited by your device's available memory, not a limit set by this tool.

Does pausing the source pause the recording?

No — the recorder keeps running and simply captures silence while the source is paused. Stop and restart manually if you want a clean cut.
