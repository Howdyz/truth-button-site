Frequently asked questions

Is this real radio, or something simulated?

Real, live radio — this plays the actual internet streams that real FM and AM stations broadcast, pulled from Radio Browser, a free public directory of thousands of stations worldwide.

Do I need an account or app?

No. It's free, runs entirely in your browser, and doesn't require signing in, installing anything, or giving out any information.

How does the FM/AM filter work?

It's a text match on the station's name (e.g. contains "FM" or "AM") — not a verified broadcast-band field from the directory. Most US-style terrestrial simulcasts follow that naming convention, so it works well for those, but it won't catch every station worldwide.

Why is a station not playing?

Streams come from a public, community-maintained directory of independently run stations — some go offline, get geo-restricted, or shut down without notice. That's outside this tool's control; try another station.

Being straight about this: streams come from Radio Browser, a free, community-maintained public directory — this site doesn't host, upload, or control any of the audio, it's just a player for streams that already exist publicly.
