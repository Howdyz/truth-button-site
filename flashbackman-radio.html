Frequently asked questions

Is this real radio, or something simulated?

Real, live radio — this plays the actual internet streams that real FM and AM stations broadcast, pulled from Radio Browser, a free public directory of thousands of stations worldwide.

Do I need an account or app?

No. It's free, runs entirely in your browser, and doesn't require signing in, installing anything, or giving out any information.

How does the FM/AM filter work?

It's a text match on the station's name (e.g. contains "FM" or "AM") — not a verified broadcast-band field from the directory. Most US-style terrestrial simulcasts follow that naming convention, so it works well for those, but it won't catch every station worldwide.

Why is a station not playing?

Streams come from a public, community-maintained directory of independently run stations — some go offline, get geo-restricted, or shut down without notice. That's outside this tool's control; try another station.

Does streaming radio use a lot of data?

Streaming audio uses roughly the same data as any other audio stream — typically a few dozen megabytes per hour depending on the station's bitrate, similar to a music-streaming app.

Can I keep listening while using other tabs or apps?

Yes — once a station is playing, audio continues in the background as you switch tabs or use other apps, same as any other browser audio.

Why do some countries have far more stations listed than others?

Station count reflects how many independent broadcasters have registered their stream with Radio Browser's public directory, not actual radio activity in that country — some countries are simply better represented in the directory than others.

Being straight about this: streams come from Radio Browser, a free, community-maintained public directory — this site doesn't host, upload, or control any of the audio, it's just a player for streams that already exist publicly. The FM/AM filter is a text match on the station name, not a verified broadcast-band field. Some stations may be temporarily offline or geo-restricted at any time.
