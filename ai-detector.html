Frequently asked questions

How can I tell if a photo is AI-generated?

Run it through a detector like this one — it checks error-level analysis, texture uniformity, and frequency-spectrum patterns, then shows you a score and what drove it. No single check is definitive, but combined signals give a strong first-pass read.

Is this AI detector free?

Yes — completely free, no account required to analyze a file.

Does it upload my file to a server?

No. Analysis runs entirely client-side in your browser. Your file never leaves your device.

Can it check video and audio, not just photos?

Yes — the same tool supports photo, video, and audio, plus analyzing a link directly or capturing a frame straight off your screen.

Is it 100% accurate?

No — it's a heuristic demo, not a certified forensic tool. Treat results as a data point that informs your judgment, not a final verdict.

What file types are supported?

Common image formats (JPG, PNG, WebP), video formats (MP4, WebM, MOV), and audio formats (MP3, WAV, M4A).

Can I analyze a social media post directly?

Yes — paste a link, or use the screen-capture option to grab a frame from a post that's already open in another tab, without needing to download the file first.

Why did two different AI detectors give me different scores for the same file?

Heuristic detectors weigh different signals (compression artifacts, noise patterns, frequency analysis) differently, so scores are a directional read rather than a shared standard — that's true of every detector on the market, not just this one.

Being straight about this: this is a heuristic demo, not a certified detector. It's a genuinely useful first-pass check, but treat every result as a data point — not a verdict.
