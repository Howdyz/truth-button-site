Frequently asked questions

How do I find Flock Safety cameras near me?

Search by state using a crowdsourced map like DeFlock, linked from this page — there's no official public database, since Flock doesn't publish camera locations itself.

How can I check if my license plate was ever searched?

haveibeenflocked.com searches real FOIA-obtained Flock audit logs by license plate. Coverage is incomplete since not every agency releases these logs, and a hit means a search was run against your plate — not that you were stopped or investigated.

What is a Flock Safety camera?

An automated license plate reader (ALPR) camera made by Flock Safety, typically used by police departments and HOAs to log vehicles passing a location. The company operates a network of 120,000+ cameras and has drawn scrutiny over misuse cases and data-sharing practices.

How do I report a camera that isn't on the map?

Submit it to DeFlock, which feeds the live public crowdsourced map, or to DeFlock The USA's tip line. For EFF's official law-enforcement-agency tracker, submit through the Atlas of Surveillance.

Being straight about this: The Truth Button doesn't run its own camera database — this links out to real, independently-run public-interest projects (DeFlock, DeFlock The USA, EFF's Atlas of Surveillance). Their coverage is crowdsourced and incomplete, so absence from these maps doesn't mean there's no camera there, just that no one's documented it yet.
