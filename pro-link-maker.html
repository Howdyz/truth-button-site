What this actually is

  1. 1Hide a Link: upload a photo and type the link or message you want hidden inside it. The text gets encoded into the least-significant bit of each pixel, invisible to the eye, and downloads as a PNG.
  2. 2Reveal a Link: anyone with that PNG can upload it here and pull the hidden text back out — clicking the photo opens the link directly.
  3. 3Facebook Share Page: a different mechanism entirely — Facebook won't make a raw photo upload clickable, so this creates a page (hosted instantly, no setup) with proper preview tags. Paste that page's link on Facebook instead of the photo, and Facebook renders your photo as a clickable card.

Frequently asked questions

How do you hide a link inside a photo?

Upload a photo, type the URL or message you want to hide, and click Hide Link In Photo. It's encoded into the least-significant bit of each pixel's color values — invisible to the eye — and you download the result as a PNG.

Does this upload my photo anywhere?

For Hide/Reveal, no — everything happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API, and the photo never leaves your device. The separate Share Page tool is different: it hosts your photo on our server (free sign-in required) since Facebook needs a public URL to fetch, not a raw file.

Will the hidden link survive if I share the photo?

Only if the file stays an unmodified PNG. Re-saving as JPEG, resizing, screenshotting, or sending through an app that recompresses images will destroy the hidden data — download and share the exact PNG file this tool produces.

Is this secure or private encryption?

No — it's basic steganography, not encryption. Anyone with this tool (or general stego knowledge) can extract the hidden text. It's meant for fun, watermarking, or low-stakes hidden notes, not anything that needs real secrecy.

How do I make a photo clickable when I post it on Facebook?

Facebook won't let a native photo upload be clickable — it always opens its own photo viewer. Instead, use the Share Page tab: enter your destination URL and a photo (upload one right there if you don't have a URL for it), and click Create Share Page. It's hosted instantly, ready to paste into Facebook — nothing to download or host yourself.

Does the Share Page trick work on Instagram or TikTok?

No. It works on Facebook, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Slack, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, and Reddit, which all render link previews from a page's meta tags. Instagram and TikTok don't make links clickable in regular posts at all, regardless of what tags a page has — only a bio link, a Stories link sticker (Instagram), or paid ads carry an outbound link on those platforms.

Can I see or delete the share pages I've made?

Yes — once signed in, the Share Page tab shows a "My Share Pages" list below the form with every page you've created, its view count, and a delete button. Deleting one breaks its link immediately, including anywhere it's already been pasted.

Being straight about this: Hide/Reveal text is stored in the least-significant bit of each pixel — invisible to the eye, but not cryptography and not robust against re-compression, resizing, or screenshots; only the exact PNG this tool produces will decode correctly. The Share Page tool is unrelated (Open Graph/Twitter Card tags, not steganography) and works on Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Slack, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, and Reddit — but not Instagram or TikTok, which don't support clickable links in regular posts at all.
