Frequently asked questions

How do I find data centers near me?

Search by state using a directory like Data Center Map, linked from this page — an independent commercial directory of colocation and cloud facilities. It doesn't include classified, government, or many hyperscaler-owned sites that don't publicly list.

Why is it hard to find out who owns a data center?

Developers, especially for AI buildouts, routinely acquire land through shell LLCs before communities know what's being proposed. Delaware and Nevada both allow forming an LLC without disclosing its owners, and the federal beneficial-ownership disclosure requirement (Corporate Transparency Act) was rolled back in 2026.

Are data center directories complete?

No. Inventories are inherently incomplete since not every operator discloses locations, especially newer hyperscale and government-adjacent facilities.

Being straight about this: this isn't Truth Button's own database — it's a link to a real third-party directory. We can't hand you "Company X secretly owns Data Center Y" — the entire point of the shell-LLC structure, and now the federal rollback, is that ownership often isn't traceable through public records anymore. That opacity is itself the story, not a gap in our research.
